SwipeClean 32s promo - corrected Mureka prompts
===============================================

WHY THIS FILE EXISTS
--------------------
The 2026-09-07 generation put the timing directions inside the LYRICS field,
in square brackets. Mureka only treats a bracket as a structure tag when it is
one it recognises ([Intro], [Verse], [Chorus], [Bridge], [Outro]). Everything
else in brackets it treats as words, and the singer sang them.

Measured, not guessed - centre-channel (vocal) energy against the sides,
300-3000 Hz, on the returned track:

  known sung lyric lines .................. mid/side 3.7 - 5.3
  known instrumental intro ................ mid/side 1.8 - 2.3
  "[0:03.27 - four bass stabs...]" ........ mid/side 6.8   <- sung
  "[0:23]" ................................ mid/side 6.2   <- sung
  "[0:27.5 - drums out...]" ............... mid/side 5.0   <- sung
  "[0:11]" ................................ mid/side 4.6   <- sung
  "[0:17.5]" .............................. mid/side 3.5   <- sung

So: the LYRICS field gets words only. The arrangement lives in the STYLE field,
which is a description and is never sung.

The generator also ignored the 32-second length (it returned 96s and 131s).
Expect to re-cut; ask for "one short verse and a short outro, no repeats".

LYRIC CONSTRAINTS - check any regenerated lyrics against this
------------------------------------------------------------
SwipeClean is live on Google Play, so a sung claim is held to the same bar as
the store listing. Never sing:
  - "nothing ever leaves your phone" - false; the free tier links AdMob and
    RevenueCat. "Cleaning done right where you stand" is the true form.
  - "no quality loss" / "lossless" - the approved wording is "without visibly
    losing quality". "Nothing you'd ever miss" is the sung form.
  - "free" attached to Smart Clean, Convert, video compression or Compress All.
  - "AI" or "machine learning" - the detectors are MD5, dHash and
    variance-of-Laplacian.
  - Superlatives ("#1", "best", "fastest"), competitor names, iOS references.
